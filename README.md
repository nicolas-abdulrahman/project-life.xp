# 🌌 XP (Soul Upload & Real-Life RPG)

XP is an experimental life-gamification system where you upload your digital soul to the cloud and level up your core stats by completing real-world activities. Stop grinding in video games—start grinding in real life.

---

## 🚀 Concept

Your phone is the portal to your uploaded soul. Every productive hour you spend in the real world translates directly into experience points, stat points, and character progression.

- **Study** to increase your **Intelligence (INT)**
- **Work Out** to increase your **Strength (STR) & Endurance (END)**
- **Build Projects** to increase your **Dexterity (DEX) & Wisdom (WIS)**

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Game Engine:** Godot 4 (Mobile Portrait Mode: 720x1280)
- **Graphics & Motion:** Adobe After Effects
- **Target Platforms:** iOS & Android
